# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ambitious high school students exploring finance, business, economics, investing, and related careers. They use Beacon to find credible opportunities and practical learning resources without sorting through low-quality aggregators.

## Product Purpose

Beacon Finance helps students discover, evaluate, save, compare, and act on high-quality opportunities and resources. Success means students can quickly understand what is relevant, trust the source, and take a clear next step.

## Brand Personality

Premium, trustworthy, and focused. The voice is concise, editorial, direct, and encouraging without sounding juvenile or promotional.

## Anti-references

Generic student portals, school-club websites, noisy startup dashboards, dating-app swipe tropes, glowing futuristic interfaces, excessive floating cards, and mascot-led branding.

## Design Principles

1. Make serious information fast to scan.
2. Earn trust through direct sources, verification context, and precise language.
3. Use editorial hierarchy instead of decorative containers.
4. Give every screen a clear primary action and useful next step.
5. Keep Beacon's identity refined and understated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, descriptive labels, adequate contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts usable on student devices.
